Privacy Policy — Neodoku

Effective 30 July 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Neodoku, a mobile puzzle game published by Rogue Games (the “Service Provider”), together with any related services (collectively, the “Application”).

Data Controller

Rogue Games is the Data Controller responsible for processing your personal data.

  • Name: Rogue Games
  • Email: roguegames2222@gmail.com

To exercise any of your rights, or for any data protection question, contact the Data Controller using the details above.

The short version

Neodoku has no accounts, no login, and no registration. It never asks you for your name, email address or any other personal detail, and your game progress is stored on your own device. The only personal data involved is what the Application's third-party analytics and advertising components collect automatically, and those are switched off until you agree to them.

What information does the Application collect?

Information you provide

None is required. The Application has no registration and no profile. If you choose to email the Service Provider, your email address and the contents of your message are processed solely in order to answer you.

Information collected automatically

Where you have consented (see “Your choices” below), the Application's analytics and advertising components may collect:

  • Device type, model, operating system and system language
  • A device identifier and, for advertising, the device's resettable advertising ID (Google Advertising ID on Android; Identifier for Advertisers on iOS)
  • IP address, from which an approximate country or region may be derived
  • Application usage and gameplay events — for example levels started and completed, scores, session length, purchases of in-game items, and interactions with advertisements
  • Crash and performance diagnostics

If you decline, the analytics and advertising components are not started and this data is not collected. Advertisements shown in that case are non-personalised.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by the app store you bought the Application from (Google Play or the Apple App Store). The Service Provider receives confirmation that a purchase completed, so that the corresponding item can be unlocked. The Service Provider never receives or stores your payment card details, billing address or any other payment information.

Location

The Application does not collect precise location data and does not request location permission. Approximate country-level location may be inferred from your IP address by the third-party services listed below.

Legal bases for processing (GDPR)

Where the GDPR applies, the Service Provider relies on the following lawful bases:

  • Consent — for analytics and for personalised advertising. Nothing in these categories is collected before you give consent, and you may withdraw it at any time (see “Your choices”).
  • Contract performance — to deliver in-app purchases you have made and to provide the Application itself.
  • Legitimate interests — limited to keeping the Application secure and preventing fraud and abuse, where those interests are not overridden by your rights and freedoms.
  • Legal obligation — to comply with applicable law and lawful requests from authorities.

Your choices

When you first open the Application you are asked whether you consent to analytics and personalised advertising. Both are off until you opt in. Declining does not restrict any part of the game.

You can change your answer at any time in the Application's Settings screen. Withdrawing consent stops further collection; it does not undo processing that already took place lawfully before you withdrew.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's operating system settings, and you can stop all collection by uninstalling the Application.

Third-party services

The Application uses the following third-party services, each of which has its own privacy policy governing how it handles data:

  • Unity Technologies — Unity Analytics (gameplay analytics) and Unity LevelPlay / ironSource, which mediates the advertising described below
  • Google AdMob — advertising, served through LevelPlay mediation
  • AppLovin — advertising, served through LevelPlay mediation
  • Google Play Billing — processing of in-app purchases on Android
  • Apple — processing of in-app purchases on iOS, where the Application is distributed there

These providers act as processors or, in the case of the advertising networks, as independent controllers for their own advertising purposes. Where the GDPR applies and a provider processes personal data on the Service Provider's behalf, a Data Processing Agreement under Article 28 GDPR is in place.

Advertising and profiling

If you consent to personalised advertising, the advertising networks listed above may use your advertising ID and interaction data to build an interest profile and select advertisements on that basis. This is profiling within the meaning of the GDPR. It does not produce legal effects concerning you and does not similarly significantly affect you. You can decline personalised advertising, or withdraw consent later in Settings, and still use the whole game — you will simply see non-personalised advertisements instead.

The Application does not use artificial intelligence to process your data, and makes no automated decisions about you beyond the advertising selection described in this section.

Disclosure of information

The Service Provider may disclose information:

  • as required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena or similar legal process;
  • where it believes in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect its rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, investigate fraud, or respond to a government request;
  • to the trusted service providers listed above, who work on its behalf and are bound by contract.

The Service Provider does not sell your personal data for money.

International data transfers

The third-party services above may process personal data outside the European Economic Area. Where such transfers occur, they are made under a transfer mechanism recognised by Chapter V of the GDPR — an adequacy decision of the European Commission, Standard Contractual Clauses, or another applicable safeguard or derogation. Countries outside the EEA may not offer the same level of protection as the EEA.

Data retention

Personal data is kept only for as long as it is necessary for the purposes described in this policy.

  • Analytics and advertising data is held by the third-party providers listed above and is retained according to their own published retention policies, which the Service Provider does not control. Please refer to their privacy policies for the applicable periods.
  • Correspondence you send by email is kept for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and any follow-up, and then deleted.
  • Aggregated or anonymised data, which can no longer identify you, may be kept indefinitely.
  • Data required for legal compliance, such as transaction records, is kept for as long as applicable law requires.

Your game progress is stored locally on your device and is removed when you uninstall the Application.

Children

Neodoku is a general-audience puzzle game. It is not directed at children, is not designed or marketed for them, and contains no content aimed at them — but it is not age-restricted, and children may play it.

The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal data from children below the applicable age of digital consent, which is 16 in some countries and as low as 13 in others. Because analytics and personalised advertising require an affirmative opt-in that is off by default, no data in those categories is collected from any user — of any age — who has not consented.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal data, please contact roguegames2222@gmail.com and it will be deleted.

Security

The Service Provider takes reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information it processes, including limiting access to those who need it. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Data breach notification

In the event of a personal data breach posing a risk to your rights and freedoms, the Service Provider will notify the competent supervisory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, as required by the GDPR. Where the breach is likely to result in a high risk to you, you will also be notified without undue delay, with information about its nature, the categories of data affected, and the measures taken.

Your GDPR rights

  • Access — obtain a copy of the personal data held about you.
  • Rectification — have inaccurate data corrected.
  • Erasure — have your personal data deleted.
  • Restriction — limit how your data is used.
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interests, and object to direct marketing at any time.
  • Withdraw consent — at any time, in the Application's Settings screen or by email, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out beforehand.

Requests are answered within one month, extendable by up to two further months where a request is complex, as permitted by the GDPR.

If you believe your rights have been infringed you may lodge a complaint with your national Data Protection Authority. Contact details are listed at edpb.europa.eu. In the United Kingdom, the Information Commissioner's Office can be reached at ico.org.uk.

The Service Provider does not sell personal information for money. Allowing personalised advertising may constitute “sharing” for cross-context behavioural advertising under the CPRA. To opt out, decline personalised advertising when first asked, or turn it off at any time in the Application's Settings screen.
