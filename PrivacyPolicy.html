Privacy Policy — Neodoku

Effective 12 August 2026  ·  previous version effective 30 July 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Neodoku, an Android puzzle game published by Rogue Games (the “Service Provider”) and distributed through Google Play, together with any related services (collectively, the “Application”).

Data Controller

Rogue Games is the Data Controller responsible for processing your personal data.

  • Name: Rogue Games
  • Email: roguegames2222@gmail.com

To exercise any of your rights, or for any data protection question, contact the Data Controller using the details above.

The short version

Neodoku has no accounts, no login, and no registration. It never asks you for your name, email address or any other personal detail, and your game progress is stored on your own device. The only personal data involved is what the Application's third-party analytics and advertising components collect automatically. Analytics is switched off entirely unless you opt in. The game is funded by advertising, so adverts are shown either way — but if you decline, they are non-personalised, your advertising ID is withheld, and no interest profile is built about you.

What information does the Application collect?

Information you provide

None is required. The Application has no registration and no profile. If you choose to email the Service Provider, your email address and the contents of your message are processed solely in order to answer you.

Information collected automatically

Where you have consented (see “Your choices” below), the Application's analytics and advertising components may collect:

  • Device type, model, operating system and system language
  • A device identifier and, for advertising, the device's resettable Google Advertising ID
  • IP address, from which an approximate country or region may be derived
  • Application usage and gameplay events — for example levels started and completed, scores, session length, purchases of in-game items, interactions with advertisements, and whether a reminder notification was opened
  • Technical diagnostics reported by the analytics and advertising components themselves, such as SDK errors and delivery performance

What happens if you decline

Declining is a real choice and it is honoured in code, but the two components behave differently, and this policy would rather be exact than flattering:

  • Analytics is not started at all. No gameplay events, scores or session data are collected or sent. Nothing is buffered for later.
  • Advertising still runs, in a restricted mode. The Application is funded by adverts, so they continue to be shown — but the advertising SDK is told to withhold your device identifier, to treat your data as not available for sale or sharing, and to serve only non-personalised adverts. No interest profile is built about you.

Even for a non-personalised advert, the advertising network necessarily receives a minimum of technical information in order to deliver it and to prevent fraud — typically your IP address, coarse device and operating-system details, and the fact that an advert was requested. This is the irreducible minimum for showing any advert at all; it is not used to build a profile of you.

If you would prefer to see no adverts between rounds, the paid Remove Ads in-app purchase removes the interstitial adverts. Optional “watch an advert for a reward” adverts remain available to everyone, because you choose to start those yourself.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. The Service Provider receives confirmation that a purchase completed, so that the corresponding item can be unlocked. The Service Provider never receives or stores your payment card details, billing address or any other payment information.

Location

The Application does not collect precise location data and does not request location permission. Approximate country-level location may be inferred from your IP address by the third-party services listed below.

Notifications

The Application can send you a single local reminder about your daily puzzle streak. It is scheduled on your own device by the Application itself; no notification service receives your data and no message is sent from a server. On Android 13 and later you are asked for notification permission before any reminder is scheduled, and you can revoke it at any time in your device settings. If you have consented to analytics, the fact that a reminder was opened is recorded as a gameplay event.

Error log stored on your device

The Application writes errors and crash details to a plain text file in its own private storage so that a problem can be diagnosed if you choose to report one. This file never leaves your device. It is not uploaded, not transmitted to the Service Provider, and is deleted when you uninstall the Application. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated before it does.

Legal bases for processing (GDPR)

Where the GDPR applies, the Service Provider relies on the following lawful bases:

  • Consent — for analytics and for personalised advertising. Nothing in these categories is collected before you give consent, and you may withdraw it at any time (see “Your choices”).
  • Contract performance — to deliver in-app purchases you have made and to provide the Application itself.
  • Legitimate interests — limited to keeping the Application secure and preventing fraud and abuse, where those interests are not overridden by your rights and freedoms.
  • Legal obligation — to comply with applicable law and lawful requests from authorities.

Your choices

When you first open the Application you are asked a single question covering both analytics and personalised advertising. Both are off until you opt in, and no answer stored means no consent. Declining does not restrict any part of the game — every level, mode and feature remains available.

You can change your answer at any time in the Application's Settings screen. Withdrawing consent stops further collection; it does not undo processing that already took place lawfully before you withdrew.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's operating system settings, and you can stop all collection by uninstalling the Application.

Third-party services

The Application uses the following third-party services, each of which has its own privacy policy governing how it handles data:

  • Unity Technologies — in three roles: Unity Analytics (gameplay analytics), Unity LevelPlay / ironSource (advertising mediation), and Unity Ads and ironSource Ads, which are the advertising networks currently serving the adverts you see
  • Google Play Billing — processing of in-app purchases

These providers act as processors or, in the case of the advertising networks, as independent controllers for their own advertising purposes. Where the GDPR applies and a provider processes personal data on the Service Provider's behalf, a Data Processing Agreement under Article 28 GDPR is in place.

The advertising mediation platform makes it possible to add further advertising networks later. If any additional network is enabled, this list will be updated to name it before it begins serving adverts in the Application. The list above reflects the networks in use as of the effective date at the top of this page.

Advertising and profiling

If you consent to personalised advertising, the advertising networks listed above may use your advertising ID and interaction data to build an interest profile and select advertisements on that basis. This is profiling within the meaning of the GDPR. It does not produce legal effects concerning you and does not similarly significantly affect you. You can decline personalised advertising, or withdraw consent later in Settings, and still use the whole game — you will simply see non-personalised advertisements instead.

The Application does not use artificial intelligence to process your data, and makes no automated decisions about you beyond the advertising selection described in this section.

  • to the trusted service providers listed above, who work on its behalf and are bound by contract.

The Service Provider does not sell your personal data for money.

Where such transfers occur, they are made under a transfer mechanism recognised by Chapter V of the GDPR — an adequacy decision of the European Commission, Standard Contractual Clauses, or another applicable safeguard or derogation. Countries outside the EEA may not offer the same level of protection as the EEA.

Data retention

Personal data is kept only for as long as it is necessary for the purposes described in this policy.

  • Analytics and advertising data is held by the third-party providers listed above and is retained according to their own published retention policies, which the Service Provider does not control. Please refer to their privacy policies for the applicable periods.
  • Correspondence you send by email is kept for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and any follow-up, and then deleted.
  • Aggregated or anonymised data, which can no longer identify you, may be kept indefinitely.
  • Data required for legal compliance, such as transaction records, is kept for as long as applicable law requires.

Your game progress is stored locally on your device and is removed when you uninstall the Application.

Children

Neodoku is a general-audience puzzle game. It is not directed at children, is not designed or marketed for them, and contains no content aimed at them — but it is not age-restricted, and children may play it.

The Application's target audience on the app store is declared as 13 and over, and it is not enrolled in Google Play's Families programme.

The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal data from children below the applicable age of digital consent, which is 16 in some countries and as low as 13 in others. Because analytics and personalised advertising require an affirmative opt-in that is off by default, no data in those categories is collected from any user — of any age — who has not consented.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal data, please contact roguegames2222@gmail.com and it will be deleted.
